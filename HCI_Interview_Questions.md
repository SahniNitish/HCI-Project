## Core Interview Questions

### 1. Personal Finance Management Experience
**Question:** "Can you walk me through how you currently manage your personal finances? What tools or methods do you use to track your expenses and budget?"

**Purpose:** Understanding current user behavior and pain points  
**Follow-up probes:**
- What works well with your current method?
- What frustrates you the most about tracking expenses?
- How often do you review your spending?

---

### 2. Technology Adoption and Preferences
**Question:** "When you think about using a mobile app or web application for managing money, what features would be most important to you? What would make you actually use it regularly?"

**Purpose:** Identifying key functionality and motivation factors  
**Follow-up probes:**
- Have you tried any finance apps before? What was your experience?
- What would prevent you from using such an app?
- Do you prefer mobile apps or desktop/web applications?

---

### 3. AI Assistant Usability and Trust
**Question:** "This application includes an AI financial advisor that analyzes your spending and gives personalized advice. How do you feel about receiving financial advice from an AI? What would make you trust or distrust such a feature?"

**Purpose:** Understanding AI acceptance and trust factors  
**Follow-up probes:**
- Would you prefer serious, professional advice or something more casual/humorous?
- How detailed should the advice be?
- What information would you be comfortable sharing with an AI?

---

### 4. User Interface and Experience Expectations
**Question:** "Imagine you're adding a new expense to the app - you just bought coffee from Starbucks for $5. Walk me through what you would expect this process to look like. What information should the app capture automatically vs. what should you input manually?"

**Purpose:** Understanding workflow expectations and automation preferences  
**Follow-up probes:**
- How important is it for the app to automatically categorize expenses?
- What would you do if the app miscategorized an expense?
- How would you want to view your spending data (charts, lists, summaries)?

---

### 5. Privacy and Data Sharing Concerns
**Question:** "This app would have access to your financial data and spending patterns. What are your main concerns about privacy and data security? What would the app need to communicate to make you feel comfortable using it?"

**Purpose:** Identifying privacy concerns and security requirements  
**Follow-up probes:**
- Would you be willing to connect bank accounts for automatic expense tracking?
- What data should never be shared or stored?
- How should the app handle sensitive financial information?
