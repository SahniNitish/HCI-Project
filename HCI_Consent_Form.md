## Purpose of the Study

You are being invited to participate in a research study about user experience and interface design for a personal finance management application. This study is being conducted as part of an HCI course project to understand how people interact with financial technology and AI-powered features.

The purpose of this study is to:
- Understand user preferences for personal finance management tools
- Evaluate the usability and acceptability of AI financial advisors
- Gather feedback on user interface design and functionality
- Identify potential improvements to the application

---

## What Will Happen During the Study

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:
- Participate in a 15-20 minute semi-structured interview
- Answer questions about your current financial management habits
- Provide feedback on proposed app features and functionality
- Share your opinions about AI-powered financial advice
- Discuss privacy and security concerns related to financial apps

**No actual financial data or personal financial information will be collected.**

---

## Voluntary Participation

Your participation in this study is **completely voluntary**. You may:
- Choose not to participate without any consequences
- Skip any questions you don't want to answer
- Stop the interview at any time
- Withdraw from the study at any point

---

## Confidentiality and Privacy

Your privacy will be protected in the following ways:

**Data Collection:**
- Only general opinions and preferences will be recorded
- No personal financial information will be collected
- No bank account details or actual financial data will be requested

**Data Storage:**
- Interview notes will be anonymized (no names or identifying information)
- Data will be stored securely and only accessible to the researcher
- All data will be deleted after the completion of the course project

**Data Use:**
- Information will only be used for this HCI course project
- Results may be included in academic presentations or reports
- Individual responses will remain anonymous
- No personally identifiable information will be shared
